Exterior free-boundary minimal surfaces of every topological type with at least two ends
Authors: Igor Kozyrev
First: 2026-10-13T17:55:01+00:00 · Latest: 2026-10-13T17:55:01+00:00
Comments: 39 pages, 4 figures. Interactive versions of the figures and Surface Evolver approximations: https://surfaces.igorkozyrev.com
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
An exterior free-boundary minimal surface is a properly immersed minimal surface in the complement of the open unit ball of $\mathbb{R}^3$ whose boundary lies on the unit sphere and meets it orthogonally. We prove that for all integers $\ell\geq2$, $b\geq1$ and $γ\geq0$ there is a connected, orientable, properly embedded exterior free-boundary minimal surface with $\ell$ ends, $b$ boundary components and genus $γ$. Its total curvature is finite, and its ends are parallel catenoidal ends of multiplicity one. By the theorem of Mazet and Mendes, a surface of this kind with one end is a rotational annulus. Hence the topological types of such embedded surfaces are exactly the annulus with one end and all types with at least two ends. There are two gluing constructions. For $\ell\geq3$ we stack $\ell$ sheets over a stable catenoidal annulus that meets the sphere along a circle of radius $4/5$, and we join adjacent sheets by half-catenoidal bridges sitting on the sphere. The sheets are small normal graphs over this annulus whose displacements contain different multiples of the Jacobi field of the family of such annuli, so their boundary circles and the logarithmic growth of their ends differ slightly. For $\ell=2$ we double a catenoidal annulus near the critical angle $π/4$, where it acquires a bounded rotationally invariant Jacobi field; the distance to the critical annulus is coupled to the size of the bridges. In gluing constructions inside the unit ball that double or stack a compact surface, the bridges themselves must balance the sheets, which forces many bridges. Here the logarithmic growth of the ends absorbs this balancing. A fixed number of bridges therefore suffices, and every topological type is reached, including the small ones.

Abstract
It is proved that every Borel ensemble of pure states on a separable bipartite Hilbert space with finite average entanglement admits a countable ensemble with the same barycenter and arbitrarily small additional average entanglement. Consequently, the discrete and continuous versions of the Entanglement of Formation coincide on the whole space of states of infinite-dimensional bipartite quantum system (with infinite values allowed). The main idea of the proof (realized with the AI-assistance) is the identification of a Borel ensemble of pure bipartite states with a Borel probability measure on the unit sphere of the space of Hilbert--Schmidt operators followed by dividing the support of this measure into appropriate cells and replacing each cell by its conditional barycenter. The resulting countable ensemble is obtained by a recursive construction.

Abstract
Let $Γ<SO(4)$ be a nontrivial finite group acting freely on $S^3$, and let $M=S^3/Γ$. We prove that every smooth conformal class $C$ on $M$ satisfies \[ Y(M,C)\le |Γ|^{-2/3}Y(S^3), \] with equality for the quotient-round conformal class. Consequently, \[ σ(M)=|Γ|^{-2/3}Y(S^3). \] Our approach uses four-dimensional Poincaré--Einstein fillings of the ball. We first establish compactness for fillings whose conformal infinities range over a compact family of positive-scalar-curvature metrics. Since compactness alone does not guarantee continuation through degenerate Einstein metrics, we use equivariant Fredholm parity to obtain a filling with the prescribed boundary conformal class and group action. A common interior fixed point then makes the Li--Qing--Shi boundary test function invariant, and its descent to $M$ gives the sharp covering factor.

Abstract
We study the invariant rank-four deformed Hermitian--Yang--Mills equation associated with the chain $\mathcal{O}(1)\to\mathcal{O}(2)^{\oplus2}\to\mathcal{O}(3)$ over $\mathbb P^1$, with vertical weights $(4,2,0)$ and a fixed product Kähler class and phase. For arbitrary linear arrows $ψ=Bz$ and $φ=(Az)^T$, smooth strictly calibrated reduced solutions exist exactly when $A$ is invertible and $z^TA^TBz$ is not identically zero. The same condition characterizes stability, semistability and polystability in the specified equivariant coherent category. The classification includes all eleven nonempty coefficient strata and both coincident and distinct divisor points on the rank-one lower boundary. The analytic proof retains the ordered, noncommuting middle equation. After constructing positive-parameter solutions, it rules out unbounded metric values by combining the source budgets with moving spectral tests; on the rank-one lower boundary, the divisor mass must also be retained. Global value, gradient and symbol estimates then give a smooth strictly calibrated endpoint on the original chain. No equivalence with full coherent or derived-category stability, uniqueness, or estimate uniform across changing chains is asserted.

Abstract
We associate each h-convex body in hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}^{n+1}(n\geq 2)$ with a Radon measure valued $(0,2)$-tensor on the sphere $\mathbb{S}^n$ through its horospherical function. Using spherical convolution, we prove that any h-convex body can be approximated by a sequence of smooth strictly h-convex bodies in the Hausdorff sense. This approximation result extends the inequality parts of the Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality and the modified Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality to general h-convex bodies, but equality rigidity cannot be obtained directly. We prove that the equality holds in the Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality and the modified Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality if and only if the h-convex bodies are geodesic balls. For the Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality, we establish the Hausdorff stability independent of curvature bounds described by the Alexandrov-Fenchel deficits for h-convex bodies, which implies the equality rigidity of the Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality. For the modified Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality, we introduce a class of globally constrained $k$-th modified quermassintegrals preserving flows with general h-convex initial hypersurfaces to obtain the equality rigidity. Moreover, we prove the flows above preserve h-convexity, make the $(k+1)$-th modified quermassintegrals non-increasing and converge to geodesic spheres. We prove a rigidity theorem for modified area measures for h-convex bodies and use this rigidity theorem to characterize the geodesic balls via modified support measures for h-convex bodies, from which we prove that the $(k+1)$-th modified quermassintegrals strictly decrease in a short time along the flows with non-spherical initial hypersurfaces. Then we obtain the equality rigidity of the modified Alexandrov-Fenchel inequality.

Abstract
We construct Schauder bases of $L^p(\mathbb{R})$ with significant structural constraints on their elements. First, we construct non-negative Schauder bases of $L^p(\mathbb{R})$ for every $1 \leq p < \infty$. Although such bases cannot be unconditional, their basis constants can be made arbitrarily close to one. By duality, we also obtain bases with positive biorthogonal functionals and the same bounds for all $1\leq p<\infty$. Finally, we prove that $L^p(\mathbb{R})$ admits a Schauder basis consisting of translates of a single function for every $2\le p<\infty$. The generating function $f$ can be chosen to be the restriction to $\mathbb{R}$ of an entire function. For $p>2$, the translation parameters can be chosen from any prescribed unbounded subset of $\mathbb{R}$, a conclusion that fails for $p=2$.

Abstract
For a positive real lacunary sequence and any probability measure $μ$ with polynomial Fourier decay, we determine, for $μ$-almost every $x$, the asymptotic maximal volume of convex bodies avoiding the first $N$ dilates of $x$ modulo $\mathbb Z^d$. Among homothets of a fixed convex body, the maximal volume is asymptotic to $(\log N)/N$. When all convex bodies in the unit cube are allowed, it is asymptotic to $d(\log N)/N$. We also show, by constructing a one-dimensional counterexample, that lacunarity cannot be replaced by sparsity on sublacunary scales.

Abstract
In general, the capillarity energy outside an unbounded obstacle may fail to admit volume-constrained minimizers, as infimizing sequences may escape to infinity. We study this minimization problem outside a closed convex obstacle $C$. Via a $Γ$-convergence result for the capillarity energies outside sequences of closed convex obstacles converging in the Hausdorff sense, we prove that the infimum of the functional is attained by a generalized minimizer, namely, a finite union of regions, each of which minimizes the capillarity energy outside a limit of translations of $C$ in its volume class, with the total volume satisfying the prescribed constraint. Each generalized minimizer can be suitably approximated by an infimizing sequence, thus providing a description of its asymptotic behaviour. As an application, we study the isoperimetric profile associated with the capillarity energy and investigate how the asymptotic geometry of the obstacle affects both this profile and generalized minimizers and their connectedness.

Abstract

Such an approximate isometry is uniformly approximated by a Wigner symmetry (a unitary or antiunitary conjugation), with error at most $9\varepsilon/2$ in the trace metric and $2\sqrt2\varepsilon$ in the Bures metric, even if it does not fix zero. For maps fixing zero, the trace bound improves to $3\varepsilon$ even under the hypothesis of $δ$-surjectivity, independently of $δ$. In infinite dimensions, we construct spectrum-preserving homeomorphisms of the full state space, including mixed states, whose distortions tend to zero in both metrics but which stay a fixed uniform distance from every Wigner symmetry. For state spaces, stability holds in each fixed finite dimension with a dimension-dependent modulus tending to zero as $\varepsilon\to0$, but no such modulus can be chosen independently of dimension.

Abstract
We consider the image of the comparison homomorphism from bounded cohomology to ordinary cohomology. Its dimension is called the bounded Betti number. In our earlier work we proved that the total bounded Betti number of a closed Riemannian $n$-manifold satisfying $\Ric\geq -(n-1)$ and $\Diam\leq D$ is bounded by a constant depending only on $n$ and $D$. The original proof used the amenability of the images of sufficiently small local fundamental groups. Since that work was written, Kapovitch and Wilking proved Gromov's generalized Margulis lemma under a lower Ricci curvature bound. Their theorem gives the stronger conclusion that these local groups are uniformly virtually nilpotent. We explain how their result supplies a direct modern input for the bounded Betti number theorem. We also discuss the topological meaning of the bounded part of ordinary cohomology and its relation to the $\ell^1$-seminorm on homology.

Abstract
We prove that the renormalized volume between the two Epstein surfaces associated with a Weil--Petersson quasicircle is continuous with respect to the Weil--Petersson topology.
