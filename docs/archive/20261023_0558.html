Generalized Kahler BHE threefolds
Authors: Beatrice Brienza
First: 2026-10-21T17:58:54+00:00 · Latest: 2026-10-21T17:58:54+00:00
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study the notion of generalized Kahler Bismut--Hermitian--Einstein (GKBHE) manifolds, namely generalized Kähler manifolds for which both Bismut Ricci forms vanish, and we develop a theory for such structures on compact threefolds. We organize the analysis according to the horizontal rank, an invariant taking values in $\{0,1,2\}$ that measures the relative position of the canonical foliations associated with the two Hermitian structures. We characterize the case of horizontal rank $0$ (which corresponds to Kähler Ricci flat geometry) and describe the case of horizontal rank $1$, revealing its close interplay with the steady $ν$-soliton equation in Kähler geometry. This description also provides a tool for constructing new compact GKBHE threefolds.

First: 2025-05-12T21:19:28+00:00 · Latest: 2026-10-21T17:41:11+00:00
Comments: 115 pages. v2: Minor corrections and clarifications, including the description of the min-max construction and the presentation of the stress-energy tensor preceding Lemma 5.28. References and acknowledgments updated. Main results unchanged
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
Given a constant $λ>0$, for any parameter $\varepsilon>0$ we study critical points of the Yang--Mills--Higgs energy \[ \mathcal{Y}_{\varepsilon}(\nabla,Φ) = \int_M \left(\varepsilon^2|F_{\nabla}|^2 + |\nablaΦ|^2 + \fracλ{4\varepsilon^2}(1-|Φ|^2)^2\right)\mathrm{vol}_g, \] defined for pairs $(\nabla,Φ)$, where $\nabla$ is a connection on an $SU(2)$-bundle over an oriented Riemannian $3$-manifold $(M^3, g)$, and $Φ$ a section of the associated adjoint bundle. When $M$ is closed, we use a $3$-parameter min-max construction to produce, for $\varepsilon\ll_M 1$, non-trivial critical points in the energy regime \[ 1 \lesssim_λ\varepsilon^{-1}\mathcal{Y}_{\varepsilon}(\nabla_{\varepsilon},Φ_{\varepsilon}) \lesssim_{λ, M} 1. \] When $b_1(M)=0$, these critical points are irreducible: $\nabla_{\varepsilon}Φ_{\varepsilon}\neq 0$. Next, assuming $M$ has bounded geometry (not necessarily compact), and given critical points with $\varepsilon^{-1}\mathcal{Y}_{\varepsilon}(\nabla_{\varepsilon}, Φ_{\varepsilon})$ uniformly bounded, we show that as $\varepsilon\to 0$, the energy measures $\varepsilon^{-1}e_{\varepsilon}(\nabla_{\varepsilon}, Φ_{\varepsilon}) \mathrm{vol}_{g}$ converge subsequentially to \[ |h|^2 \mathrm{vol}_g + \sum_{x \in S}Θ(x)δ_{x}, \] where $h$ is an $L^2$ harmonic $1$-form, $S$ a finite set and each $Θ(x)$ equals the energy of a finite collection of $\mathcal{Y}_{1}$-critical points on $\mathbb{R}^3$. Finally, the estimates involved also lead to an energy gap for critical points on $3$-manifolds with bounded geometry. As a byproduct of our results, we deduce the existence of non-trivial $\mathcal{Y}_{1}$-critical points over $\mathbb{R}^3$ for any $λ>0$.

Blaschke Conjecture and Complex Geometry
Authors: Kyobeom Song
First: 2026-09-20T19:18:35+00:00 · Latest: 2026-10-21T17:20:38+00:00
Comments: 24 pages, comments very welcome
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We provide a complex-geometric approach to the Blaschke conjecture, i.e., that a manifold whose injectivity radius equals its diameter is isometric to a compact rank-one symmetric space (CROSS). In particular, we introduce the great quadric fibration, a complex analogue of the great sphere fibration. Requiring its total space to be a complex submanifold simultaneously explains many properties that a Blaschke manifold is expected to possess, including its Clifford structure, Hopf fibration, and diffeomorphism class. Furthermore, the great quadric bundle coincides with the variety of minimal rational tangents (VMRT) of the ambient Fano variety in the standard CROSS cases, and we explain this through a bend-and-break argument in the setting of the Blaschke conjecture under a suitable complexification assumption. Combining this with Hwang-Mok VMRT recognition, we prove the Blaschke conjecture for manifolds admitting an adapted complex structure on their entire tangent bundles.

Abstract
We study unitary similarity of dual complex matrices and dual quaternion matrices. First, we characterize unitary similarity in terms of the existence of a skew-Hermitian solution to a Sylvester-type matrix equation. For $2\times2$ dual complex matrices, we obtain a complete three-trace criterion when the standard part is nonnormal and analyze the limitations of trace-word criteria when the standard part is normal. We then prove, for every $n\geq1$, that two $n\times n$ dual quaternion matrices are unitarily similar if and only if their dual complex adjoint matrices are unitarily similar, and we extend this result to finite families. As a scalar consequence, we derive a finite Trace-word criterion when the standard part is nonreal and exhibit its failure when the standard part is real.

Abstract
We study the polytope of anti-diagonal X-rays of k x k doubly stochastic matrices, the image of the Birkhoff polytope under the map that sums the entries along each anti-diagonal. Its facets other than the coordinate facets are the minimal joint tail bounds for lattice laws of a sum of two dependent uniform random variables. We give an exact facet criterion in terms of the allowed edges of a tight graph. For rays of the underlying tropical fan, total support of the tight graph suffices for a facet but is not necessary, as an example with k=19 shows. Up to affine functions and positive scaling, the potentials that define a facet form a polytope whose vertices are its rays. Facets propagate by padding and by grid refinement under explicit hypotheses. We give infinite families of facets from no step potential, facets of quadratic height, superpolynomially many facets, and a classification of three-step potentials. Total support of a step potential is characterized by a Hall-type condition on block starts; with computer assistance, total support decides facets of step potentials for k<=26. The facet counts up to k=12 are found directly for k<=3 and, for k>=4, come from an enumeration by a provably complete algorithm; an independent exact recomputation reproduces its ray lists for 5<=k<=12.

Abstract
Static manifolds with boundary were recently introduced in the context of the problem of prescribing both the scalar curvature of a Riemannian manifold and the mean curvature of its boundary. These manifolds are also of interest from the perspective of general relativity. In this article, we introduce electrostatic manifolds with boundary as a natural generalization of static manifolds with boundary in the presence of a nonzero electric field. Abstract
Rudin's problem asks whether, in the unit ball of $\mathbb C^d$ with $d\ge2$ fixed, the Hardy space $H^2(\partial\mathbb B_d)$ admits a uniformly bounded orthonormal basis consisting of homogeneous holomorphic polynomials. For $d=2$ and $d=3$ the answer is due to Bourgain, but the general-dimensional case has remained open. This paper gives an affirmative answer to Rudin's problem in every fixed dimension. More precisely, we prove that there is an orthonormal basis consisting of homogeneous polynomials $\{p_j\}_{j=1}^\infty$ of $H^2(\partial\mathbb B_d)$ such that $$\|p_j\|_{L^\infty(\partial\mathbb B_d)}\leq \exp\left(\frac12d\log d+O(d)\right).$$ Here the implicit constant in $O(d)$ is independent of $d$ and $j$.

Abstract

Comments: 33 pages, 4 figures

Abstract
Finite Toeplitz truncation does not, in general, preserve multiplication. For products of $m$ $N\times N$ tridiagonal Toeplitz matrices with symbols $z+z^{-1}+a_h$, we make the two boundary corrections in Widom's product formula for finite sections explicit. The method of images gives two Hankel corrections when $m\le N+2$, a sharp uniform degree bound. For $N\ge m-1$, the ratio of the determinant of the matrix product to the Toeplitz determinant with the product symbol reduces, whenever defined, to a determinant of order $2(m-1)$, which reversal symmetry splits into two determinants of order $m-1$. A rectangular Schur polynomial gives an exact formula for the Toeplitz determinant with the product symbol, including when roots coincide. For symbols nonzero on the unit circle, classical identities for rational symbols give an exact $m\times m$ determinant formula for the ratio, with an explicit confluent form for repeated roots. With $ρ_h$ the zero of $z+z^{-1}+a_h$ in $|z|<1$, the limit is $\prod_{i<j}(1-ρ_iρ_j)^2$ as $N\to\infty$ at fixed $m,a_h$. For rectangular domino tilings, these formulas compare the classical Kasteleyn--Temperley--Fisher product with the banded Toeplitz determinant formed from the product symbol. We obtain the first exponentially small correction at fixed width and relate this comparison to the Toeplitz determinant furnished by the dual Cauchy identity.

Abstract
A complex Monge-Ampère equation for differential $(p,p)$-forms is introduced on compact Kähler manifolds. Abstract

Abstract
We introduce and prove a new conformally invariant Chern--Simons--Sobolev inequality, and classify all optimizers, which are the Hopf map $π:\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}\to\mathbb{CP}^n$ composed with a symplectic diffeomorphism of $\mathbb{CP}^n$ and an orientation-preserving conformal transformation of $\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}$. The proof uses the sharp curl-Sobolev inequality and a rigidity argument for its equality case. Motivated by the main result, we also introduce the spherical Radu--Tchrakian--Yang functional, and prove the global minimality of the Hopf map for radii in a neighborhood of $((n!)^2/n)^{1/(4n-4)}$, and characterize all minimizers. We also prove local instability for sufficiently small and sufficiently large radii, with explicit lower bounds for the Morse index.

Abstract
We study the evolution of the total isoperimetric profile of a planar convex body under volume-preserving Minkowski regularization. Several scalar geometric quantities are known to be monotone along this flow; we prove that the total isoperimetric profile is monotone as well, and characterize the equality cases. We also establish an explicit reparametrization law, by which, for nonnegative times, the profile of the regularized body is determined by that of the initial one, and the order of profiles of bodies of equal area is preserved.
