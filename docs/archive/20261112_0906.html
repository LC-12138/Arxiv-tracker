Gradient Estimates for Singular-Degenerate Elliptic Equations with Solution-Dependent Coefficients
Authors: Van Le, Tuoc Phan
First: 2026-11-10T18:56:31+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T18:56:31+00:00
Comments: 40 pages, comments are welcome
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study a conormal boundary value problem for a class of singular-degenerate quasilinear elliptic equations in divergence form in bounded domains. The leading terms are measurable in the spatial variable and depend nonlinearly on both the solution and its gradient. The singularity or degeneracy is governed by a power of the distance to the domain boundary, with exponent greater than negative one. In particular, the corresponding weights need not belong to the Muckenhoupt $A_2$ class. Assuming that the weighted mean oscillations of the leading terms in the spatial variable are sufficiently small on small balls and that the domains have Lipschitz boundaries with sufficiently small Lipschitz constants, we establish global weighted $W^{1,p}$-estimates for weak solutions with bounded weighted mean oscillation. Our results extend recent estimates for equations whose leading terms are independent of the solution itself.

Abstract
We find \emph{exotic} connected components of Anosov representations of surface groups in the higher rank group $G=G_2'$ that are neither $ι$-Fuchsian deformations nor higher rank Teichmüller spaces. These representations are found through the non-abelian Hodge correspondence via $G_2'$-Higgs bundles that we call \emph{$α$-bundles} and \emph{$β$-bundles}, which are particular cyclic Higgs bundles in the Slodowy slices of the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$-triples associated to the simple roots $α$ and $β$ of $\mathfrak{g}_2^{\mathbb{C}}$. We first prove that (most) $β$-bundles that are also Hodge bundles are $P_β$-Anosov and $α$-bundles are $P_α$-Anosov. The strategy employed is new, geometric, and general. For $σ\in \{α, β\}$, we then show the locally homogeneous $(G_2', G_2'/P_σ)$-manifold ${\mathscr{M}}_σ(ρ)$ recently described in arXiv:2510.12757, for the representation $ρ:π_1S\rightarrow G_2'$ associated to a $σ$-bundle, coincides up to finite cover with the corresponding geometric structure for $ρ$ defined via domains of discontinuity by Guichard-Wienhard and Kapovich-Leeb-Porti. Consequently, we find that $π_1({\mathscr{M}}_σ(ρ))$ is an invariant for the connected component of $ρ$ in the space of $P_σ$-Anosov representations. After verifying the folklore result that the $G_2'$-character variety has exactly three connected components, we conclude lower bounds for the number of connected components of $P_σ$-Anosov representations in the two non-Hitchin components, most of which are exotic.

Abstract
We prove an Allard regularity theorem for critical points of the Abelian Yang-Mills-Higgs energy at critical coupling in every ambient dimension. A new cylindrical monotonicity formula for the full excess, based on an improved Modica inequality, converts the tilt-excess decay of De Philippis-Halavati-Pigati into rigidity of nontrivial entire critical points with almost unit density. This resolves their Gibbons conjecture without a local minimising assumption. Adapting the local vortex expansion and projected linear theory of Badran-del Pino, we obtain quantitative vortex approximation and uniform interior $C^{2,α}$ estimates for the nodal sets. To pass to nonzero complex levels, we use a Hodge decomposition of the difference between the actual and approximate connections: curvature comparison controls the co-closed part, while the Coulomb condition gives estimates for the exact part and hence for the phase. In the Coulomb gauge with vanishing normal component on the outer boundary, this yields graphical $C^{2,α}$ bounds for all levels $\{u_\varepsilon=t\}$, uniformly for $|t|\leq1-b$, in an interior tubular neighbourhood of the nodal set.

Abstract
We construct an explicit family of real $m\times n$ restricted isometries with $m\to\infty$, $m/n\to 0$, $δ_k\to 0$, and $k=m^{2/3-o(1)}$. We combine Gershgorin's circle theorem with a flat RIP estimate to reduce the problem to choosing a large vertex set in a strongly regular graph with the property that every small subset spans few edges. We select this vertex set using an incidence geometric result due to Zahl. The proof only uses the entrywise absolute value of the Gram matrix despite the conventional wisdom that such proof techniques exhibit a barrier of $m^{1/2}$. We prove that this barrier is actually $m^{2/3}$, and we show that our particular construction exhibits the same barrier.

Planarity estimates and classification of ancient flows at the Andrews-Baker threshold
Authors: Francesco Ruatta
First: 2026-10-29T11:56:05+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T17:53:03+00:00
Comments: 17 pages. v2: added convexity, noncollapsing and classification of space-time proper ancient solutions (new Section 7); title, abstract and introduction updated accordingly. Comments are welcome!
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study space-time proper ancient solutions of the mean curvature flow of $n$-dimensional submanifolds of $\mathbb R^{n+p}$ that are uniformly quadratically pinched up to the Andrews-Baker threshold $\frac{4}{3n}$. We prove codimension reduction and convexity: every such solution is either a static affine $n$-plane, or a flow of hypersurfaces of a fixed affine $(n+1)$-dimensional subspace whose time slices bound convex bodies. By results of Bourni, Langford and Lynch and of Bamler and Lai, the flow is then noncollapsed, and it is a family of shrinking spheres or cylinders, an ancient oval, a bowl soliton or a flying wing, possibly times a Euclidean factor. Space-time properness replaces the finite entropy and polynomial curvature growth assumptions of Lee, Naff and Zhu. The main tool is a parabolically scale-invariant interior planarity estimate for $n\geq5$, obtained from Naff's gradient estimates through a localization argument in the spirit of Ecker and Huisken. In dimensions five and six, where these estimates do not give the gradient terms a sign, we bound them from below as in a previous work. The same localization, applied to the gradient estimate of Lynch and Nguyen and to a second order analogue, gives the derivative bounds from which convexity follows. For closed flows, we extend Lynch's planarity estimate to a larger range of exponents.

Regularity of $Q$-valued $C^1$ harmonic functions with applications to stable minimal hypersurfaces
Authors: Sidney Stanbury
First: 2026-11-10T17:52:13+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T17:52:13+00:00
Comments: 36 pages
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We show that the derivative of a $Q$-valued $C^1$ function on an $n$-dimensional domain that is locally harmonic on its regular set is locally Dirichlet minimising, and that its branch set coincides with that of its derivative. As a consequence, we get that a $Q$-valued $C^1$ harmonic function is $C^{1,α}$ and that its branch set is countably $(n-2)$-rectifiable when $n\geq 3$ and discrete when $n=2$. Our methods are directly applicable to the regularity theory of stable minimal hypersurfaces. We show that a multi-valued Lipschitz function whose graph is a two-sided, properly immersed, stable minimal $n$-dimensional hypersurface with locally $\mathcal{H}^{n-2}$-finite singular set, and whose Lipschitz constant is sufficiently small is in fact multi-valued $C^{1,α}$. As a consequence, we obtain optimal regularity conclusions for a branched sheeting theorem of Bellettini for singular immersions. When $n=2$, we obtain stronger conclusions and show that locally about a branch point, a $Q$-valued $C^1$ harmonic function is the real part of a Dirichlet minimiser which is itself obtained from a finite number of holomorphic functions via classical branching, thus giving a full series expansion for the function near the branch point. Furthermore, we prove that the class of branched, two-sided, properly immersed, stable minimal surfaces with isolated singularities is compact under varifold convergence.

Bi-Hamiltonian structure and conserved quantities of the Yajima-Oikawa-Newell system
Authors: Marcos Caso-Huerta
First: 2026-11-10T17:47:48+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T17:47:48+00:00
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study the Hamiltonian and bi-Hamiltonian structure of the Yajima-Oikawa-Newell (YON) system, an integrable model of long wave-short wave interaction depending on two real parameters $α$ and $β$, which reduces to the Yajima-Oikawa system for $α=0$ and to the Newell system for $β=0$. For $α\neq0$, a constant shift of the long wave maps the YON system into the Newell system, for which two nonlocal Hamiltonian operators are known. We show that, for $α\neq0$, the system admits no local Hamiltonian operator homogeneous in its natural grading and polynomial in $β$. A nonlocal gauge transformation built on the potential of the long wave makes the first of the known operators local for a single value of its parameter: the transformed system is Hamiltonian, for every $α\neq0$ and every $β$, with respect to a local, non-degenerate Poisson operator, which is the Lie-Poisson structure of the $\mathfrak{sl}(2)$ current algebra. From a recursion operator advancing the hierarchy by two steps, given in explicit weakly nonlocal form, we construct a second, weakly nonlocal Hamiltonian structure, whose nonlocal tail is generated by the three autonomous Lie point symmetries of the system, and prove that both structures are compatible. Since the recursion operator admits no square root and the first structure has no Casimirs, the Lenard-Magri chain splits into two families, seeded by the two simplest conserved quantities. Every flow of both families is local, and all the corresponding Hamiltonians are in involution. As by-products, we relate the Lie point symmetries of the system to its conserved quantities via Noether's theorem, and obtain the conserved densities in closed form by asymptotic diagonalisation of the Lax pair.

Abstract
This paper substantially extends the results of Sasaya (2026, JFA): we propose a framework for nonlinear generalizations of regular Dirichlet forms and prove that each energy in this framework has a largest strongly local part. We also prove the existence and uniqueness of canonical energy measures of this part, which satisfy convexity and locality properties and, under additional assumptions, certain chain rules in one variable. On operators with infinitely many spectral components
Authors: Janko Bračič, Marko Kandić
First: 2026-11-10T17:43:04+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T17:43:04+00:00
Comments: 28 pages
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
For a unital complex Banach algebra $\mathscr A$ and $n\in\mathbb N$, let $\mathcal C_n(\mathscr A)$ be the set of all elements whose spectrum has at least $n$ connected components, and let $\mathcal C_\infty(\mathscr A)=\bigcap_{n\in\mathbb N}\mathcal C_n(\mathscr A)$. We prove that $\mathcal C_\infty(\mathscr A)\neq\varnothing$ if and only if $\mathscr A$ contains a sequence of non-zero pairwise orthogonal idempotents, and that the number of components of a spectrum is always finite, $\aleph_0$ or $\mathfrak c$. This allows us to extend the spectral depth $d_σ(\mathscr A)$ to a cardinal-valued invariant with values in $\mathbb N\cup\{\aleph_0,\mathfrak c\}$. We show that $d_σ(\mathscr A)=\mathfrak c$ precisely when $\mathscr A$ contains a dyadic family of idempotents indexed by the full binary tree. In that case, some element has spectrum homeomorphic to the Cantor space $\mathbf 2^{\mathbb N}$. The supremum defining $d_σ(\mathscr A)$ is attained when the spectral classes of $\mathscr A$ are dense and when $\mathscr A$ is commutative, but not in general. We construct a unital complex Banach algebra with $d_σ(\mathscr A)=\aleph_0$ and $\mathcal C_\infty(\mathscr A)=\varnothing$. If $Δ$ is the character space of a commutative $\mathscr A$ and $π_0(Δ)$ is the space of all components of $Δ$, then $d_σ(\mathscr A)=\card\bigl(π_0(Δ)\bigr)$ when $π_0(Δ)$ is finite, $d_σ(\mathscr A)=\aleph_0$ when $π_0(Δ)$ is infinite and scattered, and $d_σ(\mathscr A)=\mathfrak c$ when $π_0(Δ)$ is not scattered. We also prove that the spectral depth is additive with respect to finite direct sums and that the spectral classes are invariant under injective holomorphic functions.

Abstract
Seven-dimensional supergravity poses a uniquely intricate supersymmetry problem, combining topologically massive higher-form gauge dynamics, distinct Clifford algebra properties, and rich geometric structures. We first establish the complete formulation of the maximal Cartan-gauged theory in terms of the gauge-invariant higher-form flux, including both the bosonic action and supersymmetry variations. This formulation, which also makes the smooth ungauged limit manifest, is achieved by requiring a consistent truncation to the minimal Cartan-gauged theory in the equal-charge limit. We construct the complete bilinear system for a timelike Killing vector, where additional internal orientation of the symplectic-Majorana spinor arises already in the scalar sector, uniquely among pure minimal ungauged theories. In the equal-charge sector, we decouple the algebraic constraints into SU$(3)$-isotypic components, and analyse the differential bilinear relations, determining the permitted dilaton gradient, fluxes, fibration twist and intrinsic torsion of the base. We prove that with gauging and active Cartan flux, the complex scalar bilinear vanishes identically. We also construct a Killing gauge for the higher-form potential compatible with the self-duality condition. We show that with gauging but no active Cartan flux, the Killing vector also acts as a generator of the gauged R-symmetry, and the base structure is enlarged from SU$(3)$ to U$(3)$. We find that BPS-flow solutions realize this setting. With supersymmetry, we isolate a single independent component of the 3-form equation across an exhaustive four-class partition for both theories within this setup. Applied to known and proposed solutions in both theories, this classification exposes unexplored sectors and provides an accessible toolbox for constructing and testing solutions and developing new geometric and holographic applications.

Abstract
A theorem of Balka and Keleti implies that on any complete separable metric space $\mathbb{X}$, every locally finite Borel measure $μ$ of packing dimension strictly less than $m$ is "Euclidean-like" insofar as there exist Lipschitz maps $f_i:E_i\subset\mathbb{R}^m\rightarrow\mathbb{X}$ such that $μ(\mathbb{X}\setminus\bigcup_1^\infty f_i(E_i))=0$. An ongoing challenge in geometric measure theory is to understand when this property, called $m$-rectifiability, holds for measures of packing dimension $m$. Towards this goal, we prove that every complete Ahlfors $q$-regular metric space with $q\geq m$ supports $m$-rectifiable doubling measures of packing dimension $m$. The proof of existence of such measures is new even when $\mathbb{X}=\mathbb{R}^n$ and $n>m$. More generally, on every complete Ahlfors $q$-regular metric space with $q>m-1$, we construct $m$-rectifiable, purely $(m-1)$-unrectifiable measures with any prescribed exact Hausdorff and packing dimensions $0\leq s_H\leq s_P\leq q$ with $m-1\leq s_P\leq m$, which are doubling when $s_H>0$. This verifies a conjecture of Badger and Schul. The design of the measures relies on metric cubes with variable scaling, in which the ratio of consecutive side lengths tends to infinity but jumps only on a sparse set of generations, together with Badger and Schul's square packing construction of Lipschitz maps.

Abstract
In the Euclidean plane, Thales's theorem is equivalent to the statement that a quadrilateral with two opposite right angles has a circumscribed circle. We generalize the latter to higher dimensions: a convex polytope with two distinguished vertices, the \emph{poles}, such that the faces spanned by the two poles are perpendicular, is inscribed in the sphere whose diameter joins its poles. These \emph{orthogonally bipolar} polytopes are exactly the bipolar polytopes of a companion paper that arise as cutouts by orthogonal projections. As an application we show that for a polytope $P$ and a point $B$, the balls with diameters $[V,B]$, $V$ a vertex of $P$, cover $P$, and that the power diagram of their boundary spheres refines this covering to a tiling. Further, we investigate the properties of orthogonally bipolar polytopes, their characterization, and their application to tilings: in dimension three, the cutouts of $P$ by the orthogonal projections of $B$ onto its faces tile $P$ by orthogonally bipolar polytopes exactly when every such projection lies in the relative interior of its face. Finally, we propose an interpretation of the topic in political science.

Abstract
We prove an expansion in $1/g$ of the Weil--Petersson volumes $V_{g,n}$ that is uniform over all cusp counts, from fixed $n$ to arbitrarily large $n/g$. An explicit leading term and single-factorial remainder bounds give exponentially small relative errors after truncation at a suitable order proportional to $g$. A positive recursion along the string flow connects these volumes to pure $ψ$-class intersection numbers, where Painlevé~I fixes the normalization; uniform coefficient extraction then recovers individual volumes. We determine Zograf's constant $C=1/\sqrtπ$ and resolve Mirzakhani's two questions on volume ratios for growing cusp counts and adjacent coefficient ratios uniformly over all degrees. As $λ\to\infty$, these limits approach the infinite Weil--Petersson sphere of Budd and Curien. We prove their parabolicity conjecture and, by a growing-radius coupling, answer their scaling-limit question: after deep cusps are trimmed, the rescaled infinite sphere converges to the Brownian plane.

Abstract
Let M be a complete Riemannian manifold satisfying the doubling volume condition for geodesic balls and L^q scaled Poincar{é} inequalities on suitable remote balls for some q<2. We prove the inequality $||Δ^{1/2}f||\_p\lesssim ||\nabla f||\_p$ for all p $\in$ (q, 2], which generalizes previous results due to Auscher and Coulhon. Our conclusion applies, in particular, when M has a finite number of Euclidean ends. The proof strongly relies on Hardy inequalities, which are also new in this context and of independent interest.

$\varphi$-symmetric Sasakian Lie algebras
Authors: Adrián Andrada, Alberth J. Núñez Sullca
First: 2026-11-10T15:34:00+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T15:34:00+00:00
Comments: 21 pages
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study Sasakian Lie algebras with trivial centre for which the adjoint operator $\mathrm{ad}_ξ$ associated to the Reeb vector field $ξ$ has two-dimensional image. Their structure is governed by a Kähler-exact Lie algebra $\mathfrak{h}_1$ and a vector $H_0\in\mathfrak{h}_1$, and the cases $H_0=0$ and $H_0\neq0$ are of a different nature. For $H_0=0$ we prove that the Sasakian structure is $\varphi$-symmetric if and only if $\mathfrak{h}_1$ is locally symmetric. For $H_0\neq0$ and $\mathfrak{h}_1$ solvable, we prove that it is $\varphi$-symmetric if and only if $\mathfrak{h}_1$ is locally symmetric and a natural two-form $ρ_0$ on $\mathfrak{h}_1$ determined by $H_0$ is parallel. The latter condition is equivalent to the vanishing of the mixed root spaces attached to the distinguished root selected by $H_0$ in the associated normal $j$-algebra. As a consequence, every Sasakian Lie algebra in this class of dimension at most seven is $\varphi$-symmetric, which extends a result of Loiudice and Lotta in dimension five. This bound is sharp: in dimension nine we exhibit a Sasakian structure which is not $\varphi$-symmetric, even though its Kähler base is locally symmetric.
Summary / 总结
We study Sasakian Lie algebras with trivial centre for which the adjoint operator $\mathrm{ad}_ξ$ associated to the Reeb vector field $ξ$ has two-dimensional image.

Banded Totally Positive Matrices and Positive Bidiagonal Factorizations: Existence, Stability, and Toeplitz Orthogonality
Authors: Manuel Mañas
First: 2026-10-06T15:46:15+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T15:14:11+00:00
Comments: 142 pages. Existence, analytic dependence with fixed or variable initial entries, norm interiority, Toeplitz orthogonality and convergent-tail characteristic zeros. Verification package: https://github.com/ManuelManas/btp-pbf-verification/releases/tag/final
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2 · Code3
Abstract
A positive bidiagonal factorization of a banded totally positive matrix expresses it as a product of unit lower and upper bidiagonal factors with positive off-diagonal entries and a positive diagonal matrix. In finite dimension such factorizations always exist, with parameter domains defined by polynomial inequalities. In the semi-infinite case, existence is equivalent to convergence of explicit positive series. Every bounded banded totally positive matrix is an operator-norm limit of matrices admitting these factorizations, through perturbations confined to a fixed initial block. Necessary and sufficient criteria characterize analytic dependence of prescribed factors on perturbations. With fixed initial entries, a relative entrywise criterion allows unbounded factors with different growth rates. For uniformly positive bounded factors with variable initial entries, the criterion involves every pair of factors on each triangular side and the signed triangular inverses. The dimensions of the local factorization families are determined. Normalized LDU factors depend analytically at every interior point of the factorization class. For banded totally positive Toeplitz matrices, a stable positive bidiagonal factorization exists if and only if the symbol polynomial has distinct roots. Root formulas give two polynomial vector families with mixed-type multiple orthogonality for a rectangular matrix of positive measures. One factorization simultaneously attains the smallest possible first off-diagonal entries of both normalization matrices. The weighted linear forms are independent of the factorization. Their Chebyshev expansions yield asymptotic zero spacing inside the support and convergence of normalized kernels to the sine kernel after rescaling by the local zero density.

Abstract
We use metric functionals to construct a topology on metric spaces that induces $d$-weak convergence. We show that this topology is invariant under surjective isometries and characterize its convergence in terms of weak metrics. We prove that the topology is $T_1$ on every metric space. For normed linear spaces, we further show that it is Hausdorff and coarser than the classical weak topology. Finally, we construct a sequence in $C[0,1]$ that is unbounded in the sup-norm yet converges $d$-weakly, thereby correcting an assertion in an earlier work.

Abstract
In this paper, we prove path connectedness of every nonempty strict Calabi-energy sublevel and identify the infimum of the square root of the Calabi energy over smooth metrics with the infimum of the descending Mabuchi slope in the metric completion. A counterexample to the strong version of the union of uniform closed balls conjecture in dimensions three and higher
Authors: Chadi Nour
First: 2026-11-10T15:05:03+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-10T15:05:03+00:00
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
Fifteen years after its formulation and following its recent proof in the plane, we show that the strong version of the union of uniform closed balls conjecture fails in every dimension $n\geq 3$. For every $n\geq 3$, $r>0$, and $\varepsilon>0$, we construct a compact set $S\subset\mathbb{R}^n$ satisfying the interior $r$-sphere condition and containing a point that belongs to no closed ball of radius $(1/2+\varepsilon)r$ contained in $S$. Consequently, the known universal radius $r/2$ is optimal in every dimension $n\geq 3$, even for compact sets, whereas the optimal radius in the plane is $r/\sqrt{3}$. The construction uses simplices with coincident circumcenter and incenter and yields an exact formula for the largest radius of a contained ball covering the center; the regular simplex recovers the original example motivating the conjecture.

Fifteen years after its formulation and following its recent proof in the plane, we show that the strong version of the union of uniform closed balls conjecture fails in every dimension $n\geq 3$.

Abstract
In this paper, we study the orbit manifolds of free diagonal linear circle actions on \(S^{2n+1}\times S^{2n+1}\).
