Inferring geometric structure from eigenfunction triple products
Authors: Josef Greilhuber, Romain Speciel
First: 2026-11-02T18:51:11+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-02T18:51:11+00:00
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
This article presents two converses to well-known observations regarding products of eigenfunctions of the Laplacian on a closed Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$. First, if $(M,g)$ is real-analytic, the coefficients in the eigenfunction expansion of a product of eigenfunctions decay exponentially. We show that such exponential decay in fact implies that $g$ is real-analytic. Second, it is clear that if $(M,g)$ is flat, there exists an eigenbasis for which the product of any two of its elements lies in the span of at most $N = N(M,g)$ elements. We prove that any manifold with such an eigenbasis must be flat. We then construct metrics on $\mathbb{S}^7$ that share an eigenbasis with the standard metric yet are not locally homogeneous, from which we deduce that homogeneity cannot be characterized solely in terms of the multiplicative structure of eigenfunctions.

QWEP stability under twisted crossed products
Authors: Kai Zeng
First: 2026-10-02T15:09:26+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-02T18:21:10+00:00
Comments: Updating refrences. Adding aknowledgements
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We study the QWEP property of twisted crossed products and its relation to Fourier multiplier transference on noncommutative $L_p$-spaces. We show that QWEP is not preserved by crossed products of QWEP von Neumann algebras by hyperlinear groups. Our examples include trace-preserving actions of a torsion-free residually finite Kazhdan group on a diffuse abelian algebra and on the hyperfinite type II$_1$ factor. We also obtain non-QWEP crossed products with nontrivial scalar cocycles. For scalar twists, we prove that a finite trace-preserving crossed product is QWEP if and only if its ordinary crossed product and its twisted group von Neumann algebra are QWEP. In the positive direction, we establish a restriction theorem for scalar twists of regular wreath products. These results provide QWEP inputs for Fourier multiplier transference and identify a completely bounded multiplier norm obstruction to QWEP. Finally, operator-valued cocycles give finite QWEP crossed products whose acting groups are not hyperlinear.

Abstract
This paper is based on lecture notes taken and organized during the 17th Graduate Mathematics Intensive Training Program (Spring 2025), held at the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research, in which Professor Shaoming Guo lectured on "An Introduction to Fourier Decoupling Theory." The main content includes an introduction to and proofs of $\ell^2$ decoupling inequality, together with its applications in number theory, harmonic analysis, and geometric measure theory. The writing follows Professor Guo's teaching style, proving theorems in an intuitive and heuristic manner; consequently, the text contains many figures to explain the ideas at hand, while also lacking some rigor. The author hopes that readers will be able to understand, through these lecture notes, the geometric intuition hidden behind the cumbersome proofs, and thereby deepen their understanding of the relevant definitions and theorems. Rigorous proofs can be found in Professor Guo's forthcoming book, which interested readers may consult.

$C^1$-rigidity of $\mathbb{H}^{p,q}$-convex cocompact representations of Gromov hyperbolic groups
Authors: Nikolay Bogachev
First: 2026-11-02T18:02:36+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-02T18:02:36+00:00
Comments: 33 pages, comments are very welcome!
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
Let $p>3$ and let $Γ$ be a non-elementary Gromov hyperbolic group with $\partial_\inftyΓ\cong\mathbb S^{p-1}$. We prove that every $\mathbb{H}^{p,2}$-convex cocompact representation $$ ρ:Γ\longrightarrow \mathrm{PO}_{p,3} $$ whose proximal limit set $Λ_ρ\subset\partial_\infty\mathbb{H}^{p,2}$ is a $C^1$ submanifold preserves a totally geodesic copy of $\mathbb{H}^p\subset\mathbb{H}^{p,2}$. Consequently, $Γ/\ker ρ$ is virtually a cocompact lattice in $\mathrm{Isom}(\mathbb{H}^p)$. For odd $p$, we prove the same conclusion for every signature $(p,q)$ with $p>q\ge2$, under the same hypothesis $\partial_\inftyΓ\cong\mathbb S^{p-1}$. Our methods combine dynamics, differential topology, and representation theory. The key new ingredient is to study the radicals of the semidefinite forms induced on the tangent spaces of the limit set. They form a continuous vector bundle of constant rank. The tangent action at attracting fixed points gives a bound, in terms of the radical rank, on the number of eigenvalues of an orthogonal lift whose modulus exceeds one. We then use the topology of the sphere and the representation theory of real algebraic groups to exclude the remaining possibilities. In arbitrary signature with $p>q$, these arguments already imply that a representation with a $C^1$ limit set cannot be Zariski dense.

The first chiral homology group in higher genus
Authors: A. Zuevsky
First: 2026-07-27T17:54:15+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-02T18:01:50+00:00
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We extend the theory of the first chiral homology group of vertex algebras, developed by van Ekeren and Heluani for elliptic curves, to compact Riemann surfaces of arbitrary genus. Our approach realizes a genus $g$ surface by iterated self-sewing of $g$ handles onto the Riemann sphere, each governed by a sewing parameter $ρ_i$ in a punctured disc. We construct an explicit complex computing the chiral homology groups $H^{\mathrm{ch}}_0$ and $H^{\mathrm{ch}}_1$ of a vertex algebra $V$ on a genus $g$ surface with $n$ marked points, and equip it with a projectively flat connection, with an explicit central-charge anomaly, over the $g$-dimensional space of sewing parameters. We prove a genus $g$ Fourier-space Borcherds identity for the associated modified vertex operators. We show that two finiteness assumptions 1) finite dimensionality of the first Poisson homology $\HP_1(R_V)$ of the Zhu $C_2$-algebra, and 2) finite generation of a certain Koszul homology of the associated graded algebra, imply finite dimensionality of $H^{\mathrm{ch}}_1(X,V)$ for every genus $g$ and every vertex algebra $V$. Using the degeneration $ρ_i \to 0$ together with the factorization theorem of Damiolini-Gibney-Tarasca, we relate the totally degenerate limit of $H_1^{\mathrm{ch}}$ to the Hochschild homology of an iterated construction on the Zhu algebra, and deduce vanishing of the first chiral homology group in every genus for rational vertex algebras.

A proof of the spectral rigidity for complex projective space
Authors: Kefeng Liu, Hao Xu
First: 2026-11-02T18:01:00+00:00 · Latest: 2026-11-02T18:01:00+00:00
Comments: 32 pages
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We give a proof of a folklore conjecture that a compact Kähler manifold with the same scalar Laplace spectrum as complex projective space equipped with the Fubini--Study metric is holomorphically isometric to it. More generally, we prove that the spectrum of the Hodge Laplacian in any one fixed degree $p$ determines $\CP^n$ up to holomorphic isometry among compact connected Kähler manifolds, for every fixed pair of integers $(p,n)$ with $0\leq p\leq n$. Equality of a single Hodge spectrum first forces periodic geodesic flow and the integral cohomology ring of complex projective space. The ordinary first heat coefficient then identifies the total scalar curvature except on a Pell sequence of pairs. For those pairs, a clean wave-trace argument, a Krawtchouk estimate, and a mod-two projective-bundle obstruction determine the geodesic return holonomy. A canonical Kähler involution recovers a weighted first heat coefficient which is nonzero throughout the exceptional sequence. The Kobayashi--Ochiai theorem and a fixed-class second-heat-coefficient calculation complete the argument.

Abstract
The hyperplane Furstenberg problem asks how small the Hausdorff dimension of $E\subset\mathbb{R}^n$ can be when a $t$-dimensional family of affine $(n-1)$-dimensional hyperplanes meets $E$ in sets of dimension at least $s$. For $0<s\le n-1$ and $0<t\le n$, we prove the sharp bound in $\mathbb{R}^n$ $\dim_{\mathrm{H}}E\ge\mathbf{F}(s,t;n,n-1)$, where $\mathbf{F}$ is Gan's Furstenberg index proposed in prime fields. We also construct examples attaining this bound for all parameters. Our main reduction theorem, Theorem 3.1, states that for $n\ge3$, $0<s\le n-2$, and $1<t\le n$, every weighted $(s,t)$ configuration $(E,H)$ at scale $δ$ produces a dual weighted $(s,t-1)$ configuration $(P,\mathcal{T})$ inside a hyperplane $H_{y_0}\cong\mathbb{R}^{n-1}$. The reduction uses radial projection and energy estimates for Frostman measures. Iterating this reduction and applying the Csörnyei--Stull incidence theorem yields the bound.

Abstract
We establish a fixed-point framework for non-linear boundary value problems for first-order elliptic operators on vector bundles, with primary application to the spinorial Yamabe and Brezis--Nirenberg equations. We prove existence and uniqueness of an $H^1$-solution under a smallness condition that is homogeneous under amplitude and operator scaling. The method is extended to families of small domains via rescaling to a fixed reference domain.

Abstract
The Kaczmarz algorithm is an iterative projection method for reconstructing vectors in a Hilbert space from inner product data. However, sequences that are effective for this algorithm need not be frames and may lack standard frame reconstruction properties. We investigate these properties for stationary effective sequences, which were classified by Kwapień and Mycielski according to their spectral measures. In particular, we show that the corresponding Kaczmarz reconstruction series always converge unconditionally if and only if the spectral measure is atomic with finitely many point masses. We show that if the spectral measure is atomic, the stationary effective sequence and its auxiliary Parseval frame can be interchanged in the Kaczmarz reconstruction formula. Finally, we construct two stationary effective sequences with singular continuous spectral measures, one of which can be interchanged with its auxiliary Parseval frame in the reconstruction formula, and one of which cannot be interchanged.

Abstract
We establish sharp lower bounds for the lengths of balls in the universal covers of finite metric graphs. For a finite connected $d$-regular graph, with $d \geq 3$ and average edge length at most one, we show that, at every radius, the average length of balls centered at a vertex of the universal cover is at least that of a ball of the same radius in the equilateral $d$-regular tree.
