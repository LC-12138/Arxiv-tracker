Complete entropy contraction from conditional hyperboundedness

Comments: 23 pages; comments are welcome

Abstract
For finite-dimensional quantum channels compatible with a faithful conditional expectation, we show that conditional hyperboundedness and strict GNS contraction together yield a relative entropy contraction. For KMS-symmetric quantum Markov semigroups, a conditional $2\to q$ hyperboundedness at time $t_0>0$ combined with a GNS gap $λ>0$ implies a modified logarithmic Sobolev constant \[ α=\frac{λ(1-2/q)} {12(λt_0+\log M+1-2/q)},\] where $M$ is the hyperboundedness constant. As a consequence, completely bounded hyperboundedness over auxiliary systems gives complete versions of entropy contraction with the same rates.

Characteristic gluing to extremal Kerr horizons and the third law of black hole thermodynamics in vacuum
Authors: Stefan Czimek
First: 2026-10-26T17:52:47+00:00 · Latest: 2026-10-26T17:52:47+00:00
Comments: 84 pages, all comments welcome
Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
Abstract
We prove a non-perturbative, non-axisymmetric characteristic gluing theorem for the Einstein vacuum equations: every Boyer-Lindquist sphere in the domain of outer communication of a Kerr black hole, in particular every symmetry sphere of a Schwarzschild exterior, can be glued along a single outgoing null hypersurface to a horizon sphere of an extremal Kerr black hole of sufficiently large mass. The gluing is exact at both ends and matches the full sphere data, including the transversal curvature. This proves a conjecture of Kehle and Unger in the extremal case. We first raise the angular momentum to the extremal value by rotating shear pulses of non-axisymmetric gravitational radiation, and then damp the incoming data exponentially along a long non-expanding neck. At the degenerate target horizon we invert the linearised problem at all angular frequencies, using a factorisation of a fourth-order angular operator into an angular Teukolsky-Starobinsky product. As an application, we construct one-ended asymptotically flat vacuum initial data whose maximal development contains an exactly Schwarzschild apparent horizon and, at a later advanced time, an exactly extremal Kerr event horizon. This is the first rigorous counterexample to the third law of black hole thermodynamics in vacuum, and proves a second conjecture of Kehle and Unger.

Abstract
Given a positive, non-increasing sequence $a$ with sum $1$, we consider the family $\mathcal{C}_a$ of closed subsets of $[0,1]$ whose complementary open intervals have lengths given by the terms of $a$. We study which functions $θ\mapsto\overline{\dim}_{\,θ}E$ arise as the upper intermediate dimensions of sets $E\in\mathcal{C}_a$. Under a natural condition on the decay of $a$, we show that these are exactly the functions in the class introduced by Banaji and Rutar that take the value $\overline{\dim}_{\rm B}C_a$ at $θ=1$ and are bounded above by the upper intermediate dimensions of the decreasing Cantor set $C_a$. We also show that the lower bound given by the countable set $D_a$ is rigid: if it is attained at one value of $θ$, then it is attained at all larger values.

Abstract
Following Simons' seminal work in 1968, every closed, connected, embedded minimal surface in the four-sphere that is not totally geodesic has Morse index at least five. In this paper, we prove that equality holds if and only if the surface is congruent to the Veronese projective plane. Relying on recent advances by Bernstein and Ketover, this characterization settles Kusner's 1996 conjecture on the minimizers of the Willmore functional in dimension four for surfaces whose normal bundle has nonzero Euler number. This is always the case, in particular, for embeddings of nonorientable surfaces whose orientation double cover has even genus.

Abstract

Abstract
We develop a multiscale criterion for exponential decay of relative entropy in the canonical stochastic quantization of lattice field theories, the underdamped Langevin dynamics of the field and its momentum. For smooth bounded perturbations of Gaussian fields, the criterion requires the uniform operator-norm bound on the smoothed interaction Hessian along the Polchinski flow of Bauerschmidt and Bodineau to have a small $L^2$ norm in the scale variable. The decay constants depend only on this $L^2$ norm and the friction. The proof measures the transfer of momentum dissipation to position on the same scales that decompose entropy. A phase-space extension of the Polchinski flow gives Gaussian coarse-graining that commutes with free kinetic evolution. Integrating position--momentum cross terms over these scales yields a corrector bounded by entropy. A force defect accounts for changes in the averaged interaction force away from equilibrium; its evolution in scale controls the errors in the kinetic estimate. For the massive two-dimensional lattice sine--Gordon model with $0<β<4π$ and small dimensionless local coupling, the criterion gives entropy decay for all finite-entropy initial laws, uniformly in the lattice spacing and the volume. For friction proportional to the mass $m$, the rate has optimal order $m$.

Abstract
Berger and Coburn showed that boundedness of a Toeplitz operator $T_g$ on the Fock space controls the heat transform $g^{(t)}$ for $1/4<t<1$, and conjectured that $T_g$ is bounded if and only if $g^{(1/4)}$ is bounded. Looi recently disproved the forward implication by constructing a bounded $T_g$ with unbounded $g^{(1/4)}$. We prove that the reverse implication also fails, even for a real-valued admissible symbol $g$ with $g^{(1/4)}\in C_0(\mathbb{C}^n)$, and that the forward implication fails even when $T_g\in S_p$ for every finite $p>1$. Our counterexamples come from Baire category and closed graph arguments, which yield no explicit symbols. Despite this two-sided failure, the forward implication has a sharp Schatten-class form: $\|g^{(1/4)}\|_\infty\le 2^n\|T_g\|_{S_1}$ for every admissible symbol $g$ with $T_g$ trace class. We show that the constant $2^n$ is optimal and that the same estimate holds with $S_p$ in place of $S_1$ for $T_g\in S_p$ when $0<p\le1$. For $p>1$, no corresponding uniform estimate holds, even for smooth compactly supported symbols. We also determine the optimal constants in the Berger--Coburn estimates for $1/4<t\le1$. Finally, for $1\le p<\infty$, we show that the equivalence $T_g\in S_p$ if and only if $g^{(1/4)}\in L^p$ holds exactly when $p=2$, answering a question of Bauer, Coburn, and Isralowitz.

Abstract
We prove that every critical spherical catenoid in a geodesic ball of hyperbolic three-space has Morse index four and nullity two. This resolves Medvedev's index conjecture throughout the parameter range of the catenoid family. The kernel consists precisely of the normal components of rotations that preserve the center of the ball and tilt the axis of the catenoid. Abstract
In this paper we introduce a class of non-flat Lorentzian manifolds, called Lorentzian torse-forming structure manifolds (briefly, LTFSMs). This class contains Lorentzian concircular structure manifolds (briefly, LCSMs), LSP-Sasakian manifolds, LP-Sasakian manifolds with coefficient $α$, LP-$α$-Sasakian manifolds, LP-Sasakian manifolds, and LP-$α$-Kenmotsu manifolds. If the gradient of the torse-forming scalar $α$ is collinear with the torse-forming vector field $ξ$, then the Ricci operator commutes with $φ$, and the manifold reduces to an LCSM. It is known that a conformally flat LCSM is quasi-Einstein and hence is a manifold of quasi-constant curvature. Several curvature-related properties of LTFSMs are established, and their sectional curvature is determined. The existence of LTFSMs is demonstrated through several proper nontrivial examples. Abstract
For a function $ψ: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$, we define the phase-corrected gradient $\nabla_{\!\circ} ψ= |ψ|^{-1} \overlineψ\,\nablaψ$ and study the regularity of the map $ψ\mapsto \nabla_{\!\circ} ψ$ on Sobolev spaces. Since $\mathrm{Re}[\nabla_{\!\circ} ψ] = \nabla |ψ|$, the phase-corrected gradient fits naturally into the framework of nonlinear composition operators. Its imaginary part $Λ= \mathrm{Im}[\nabla_{\!\circ} ψ]$, on the other hand, plays a key role in quantum hydrodynamic models. We prove that $ψ\mapsto \nabla_{\!\circ} ψ$ is bounded and continuous from $W^{s,2}(\mathbb{R}^d)$ to $W^{s-1,2}(\mathbb{R}^d)$ for $1 \leq s < \frac32$, and we show that the threshold $s = \frac32$ is sharp. In particular, this answers an open question concerning the continuity of the map $ψ\mapsto |ψ|$ on Sobolev spaces. We also show that our results remain valid for functions that do not necessarily decay at infinity, a relevant setting in the context of Ginzburg--Landau theory. Finally, we extend our analysis to more general phase-corrected gradients of the form $f(|ψ|^{-1} ψ) \nabla ψ$, where $f: \mathbb{S}^1 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is Lipschitz, and discuss implications for certain parabolic PDEs.
